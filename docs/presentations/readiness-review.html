A private AI workstation the whole team can use

TT Station turns a Tenstorrent QuietBox 2 into a shared, secure AI service: open-weight models run on its four Blackhole chips, and people use them from a desktop app, an iPhone app, or any OpenAI-compatible tool. Models run on the box, so prompts and files stay on company hardware.
