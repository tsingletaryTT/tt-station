Can't reach your box

TT Station opens as soon as it answers. Trying again every few seconds.

What to check
  • The box is switched on, and has had a minute or two to start.
  • If it just restarted or updated, this screen goes away by itself once it's ready.
  • This computer and the box are on the same network (or the same VPN).
  • Your Wi-Fi or network cable is connected.
